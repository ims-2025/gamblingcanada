# 25 Pillar Pages — Content & SEO Build Plan

A build-ready plan for 25 new high-quality pillar pages for OnlineGamblingCanada.com. Every page is chosen to match real Canadian search demand (grounded in the Search Console export), fill a gap the site doesn't already cover, and slot into a hub-and-spoke internal-linking structure that concentrates authority on the money pages.

**Non-negotiable quality bar for every page (no exceptions):**
- **1,800–2,800 words** of genuinely useful, original content. No thin pages, no filler.
- One **H1** with the primary keyword; **6–10 H2 sections** with descriptive subheads; scannable structure.
- A **comparison table or ranked operator list** where the intent is commercial.
- A **FAQ block** (4–6 questions) with **FAQPage schema**, plus **BreadcrumbList** schema.
- **5–10 internal links** with descriptive anchor text (to sibling pillars, operator reviews and money pages), and **inbound links** from at least two existing pages.
- Unique **title ≤ 60 characters**, **meta description ≤ 155 characters**, both keyword-led.
- **E-E-A-T signals:** author byline, "last updated" date, licensing references, responsible-gambling note (19+).

---

## 1. Strategy: why pillars, and why these

Google rewards topical authority. Instead of 25 disconnected articles, we build **clusters**: a small number of broad "pillar" pages that comprehensively cover a topic, each supported by and interlinked with the reviews, guides and news the site already has. This is the hub-and-spoke model.

The GSC data pointed clearly at where demand is and where the site is weak:

Canada regulates gambling **province by province**, which is the single biggest untapped structural opportunity — provincial pages are high-intent, lower-competition, and something global brands can't easily match. Seven of the 25 pillars are provincial.

## 5. Internal linking architecture

- **Hubs link down, spokes link up and across.** `/casino` links to the casino-type and bonus pillars; each pillar links back to `/casino` and to 2–3 sibling pillars.
- **Provincial spine:** `/online-gambling-by-province` links to all 6 provincial pages + the existing `/sports-betting/{province}` pages; each provincial page links back to the hub and to `/is-online-gambling-legal-in-canada`.
- **Every pillar links to 2–4 operator reviews** relevant to its angle (fast-payout → JackpotCity; free spins → PlayOJO; Ontario → Ontario-regulated brands), strengthening the review pages too.
- **Add pillar links to the homepage and footer** so they're never more than 2 clicks deep (avoids the crawl-depth issues Screaming Frog flags).
- **Update the sitemap automatically** (the Next.js `sitemap.ts` picks up static routes; add each new slug to the static list).

## 6. Production standards (how each page is built)

- Built as Next.js App Router pages, reusing existing components: `OperatorCard` for ranked lists, the FAQ `<details>` pattern with `FAQPage` JSON-LD, `Breadcrumbs`, and the `.prose` styles — so every pillar is fast, consistent and on-brand.
- **Word count 1,800–2,800**, verified before publish (strip-tags count). No page ships thin.
- **Titles ≤ 60 chars, meta ≤ 155 chars**, primary keyword in H1 + first 100 words + one H2.
- **Schema:** FAQPage + BreadcrumbList on every pillar; ItemList where a ranked list is present.
- **E-E-A-T:** author byline (rotate Jane Williams / David Andersen / Lisa Davids), "last updated" date, licensing references, and a responsible-gambling note. The bonus, "new casinos" and provincial pages carry a visible **last-updated** date and are refreshed on a schedule.
- **Accuracy:** provincial legal/regulator facts and tax content are researched and fact-checked (YMYL topic); no fabricated statistics.

## 7. Publish & index workflow (per page)

1. Build the page + add its slug to `sitemap.ts`.
2. Run the production build to verify it compiles and clears the word-count bar.
3. Commit → Push in GitHub Desktop → Vercel auto-deploys.
4. In Search Console: confirm it's in the sitemap and **Request Indexing** for the new URL.
5. Add inbound internal links from the homepage/hub and cluster siblings.

- Clicks/CTR on the commercial pillars (bonuses, Interac, fastest payout, provincial casinos).
- Assisted internal navigation: are pillars passing clicks to reviews and money pages?
- Coverage: all 25 indexed vs submitted.
